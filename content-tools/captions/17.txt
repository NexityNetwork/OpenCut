If I had to start an AI AUTOMATION BUSINESS from scratch in 2026
Here is EXACTLY what I would do.

First, what I would NOT do.
- Learn to code for 6 months. 
- Hire developers with no validation. 
- Build an MVP and pray. 
- Waste time polishing branding. 
- Spend months perfecting features. 

Try to guess what works.
All of that is how you burn cash and quit by month 3.

Here is the actual playbook:
Step 1: Pick a market with money.
Launch a B2B sales OS. A real estate portal. A SaaS platform. An AI UGC platform. AI chat and support. Pick something where businesses are already paying people to solve the problem by hand every month.

Step 2: Start with a ready product.
Start from something that already runs instead of building from scratch. Finished apps. Source code. Viral templates. Skip the 6-month dev cycle.

Step 3: Automate core operations.
N8N for workflows. Claude for the brain. Apollo for leads. Perplexity for research. Airtable for data. Apify for scraping. These six tools run your entire backend and not one of them needs a developer.

Step 4: Launch multiple products fast.
Same platform. Same source code. Different positioning. One product becomes three offers. Test demand before you build anything custom.

Step 5: Push distribution daily.
Post 3-5 Reels DAILY. Cold outreach on LinkedIn, Instagram, and email. EVERYTHING. The product does not matter if nobody sees it. Distribution is the business.

This is the exact stack I used to test 11 products in 8 weeks. 
The ones that worked became real businesses. 
The ones that did not cost me almost nothing to test.
