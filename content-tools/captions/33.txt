Most people store knowledge in Notion and execution happens separately. You write the process down. 

Then you manually do the process.

This changes that:

Claude Code reads it, understands it, and executes it autonomously.

Here is what actually happens:
You have SOPs for client onboarding, content creation, data analysis, report generation, whatever repeatable process your business runs.

Instead of reading the doc and doing it manually, you point Claude Code at the Notion page. It reads the steps. It executes the workflow. It handles the task end to end.

The doc becomes the instruction set. 
Claude becomes the worker.

Your SOPs stop being reference materials that gather dust. They become executable systems.
