1. Building software from scratch: 6 months of development, no customers.

2. Starting from ready made AI products: no development, and something real in front of people.

Most founders choose the first path. Smart ones choose the second.

Here is what the second path looks like.

You start with products that already work
Lead routing for service businesses. 
Cold outreach agents for agencies. 
Follow up automation for sales teams. 
Support agents for SaaS. 
Invoice automation for freelancers. 
Onboarding flows for products.

Each one is pre built. Each one solves a clear problem.
Your only job is distribution.

Build a landing page. Connect Stripe. Create Instagram or TikTok content showing how the product solves the problem.

Automation handles everything else.
When you promote random tools, nobody cares. 
When you sell a focused AI solution to a specific problem, people buy.

Subscription pricing is predictable
- You price it once and it renews without you. 
- Most people land in the same narrow band. 
- A handful of sales a day compounds into a real monthly number.

No inventory. No shipping. No calls. No support team.
