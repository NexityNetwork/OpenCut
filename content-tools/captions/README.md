# The master captions

Twenty-three captions that already work, kept verbatim. They are the reference
set; spin-offs get written against them, not from scratch.

Each file is `NN.txt`. The first line is metadata, `keyword|kind|source`, and
everything after it is the caption exactly as it was written - line breaks,
arrows, double spaces, the lot. They are also loaded into the Captions tab in
monolith, which is where they get read.

## The shape they all share

Every one of them is built the same way and it is worth writing down, because
the shape is doing as much work as the words:

1. **The ask is the first line and the last line.** `↓ Comment "W" for the full
   blueprint.` opens it and closes it. Somebody who reads two lines and somebody
   who reads the whole thing both get told what to do.
2. **The arrow.** `↓` on line one points at the caption from the frame above it.
3. **Short paragraphs, one idea each.** Almost never more than three sentences,
   often one. The blank line between them is the whole formatting system.
4. **Sub-heads that are not headings.** `The architecture.` / `Here is what
   happens:` / `Step 3. Automate core operations.` A phrase and a full stop,
   sitting on its own line, doing the job of a heading without looking like one.
5. **Lists marked with `→` or `-` or `->`,** never with numbers unless the
   number means something (`Month 1`, `Step 2`, `Workflow 4`).
6. **Named things.** Tools, models, node counts, prices, timings. `43 nodes.`
   `500 leads per batch.` `GPT-4.1-mini cleans the raw data.` The specificity IS
   the credibility.
7. **A cost line near the end.** What it costs to run against what it replaces.
8. **One keyword per caption**, and it is the same word top and bottom.

## What they are NOT

They are not the runbook's caption rules. These have dollar amounts, quotes
around the keyword, and the occasional hashtag-free-but-hyped line. The runbook
rules were written for the vault pusher; these are what actually gets posted.
When the two disagree, these win, because these are the ones with numbers
attached to them.
